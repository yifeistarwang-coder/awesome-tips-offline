How to drive your research forward?

"I tested the idea we discussed last time. Here are some results. It does not work. (… awkward silence)"

Such conversations happen so many times when meetings with students. How do we move forward?

You need … a *gradient vector*, a direction and magnitude that tell you where you should explore in the vast space of possible solutions.

Reporting results of your idea only tell us … nothing. It's like evaluating the loss function with one set of parameters.

We have no way to optimize/improve it.
