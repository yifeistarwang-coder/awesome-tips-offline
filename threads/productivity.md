## How to manage your time?

原帖：<https://x.com/jbhuang0604/status/1430003911037947904> · 2021-08-24 · 共 11 条串文

For the first time in five years, I have a full-time work schedule. I thought I will trivially be 3-4 times more productive, but it turns out managing time well is so damn challenging. 😬

After watching productivity videos to procrastinate, here are what I found useful. 🧵

*Eat that frog*

If you have to eat a frog, make it the very first task you do in your day. If you have to eat three frogs a day, do not start with the small one.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1430003926179405824-1.jpg)](media/1430003926179405824-1.mp4)

*Have a plan*

If you have no idea what you will be doing 2:30 PM next Wed, you are doing it wrong. It's okay to be flexible and change the plan, but make sure that you have a plan to start with.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1430003938091323392-1.jpg)](media/1430003938091323392-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1430003938091323392-1.mp4)

*Do what you are doing*

Be aware of what the task you are doing at the present time. Multitasking is simply an illusion of rapid context switching.

![image](media/1430003942935642115-1.png)

*Treat time as space*

Make EVERYTHING you plan to do as a calendar entry (including planning the calendar) and do it at that time. Check out @deviparikh's excellent blog post.

https://deviparikh.medium.com/calendar-in-stead-of-to-do-lists-9ada86a512dd

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1430003951533965318-1.jpg)](media/1430003951533965318-1.mp4)

*Use interrupt coalescing*

Group and defer interruptions (e.g., emails, slack messages, twitter feeds) according to their urgency. Allocate a specific time slot in a day to address these interruptions.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1430003959796797441-1.jpg)](media/1430003959796797441-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1430003959796797441-1.mp4)

*Break your day down into short time chunks*

Get a timer/app, work on your task for 25 mins. Take a 5 min break and repeat. (Pomodoro Technique)

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1430003969607229444-1.jpg)](media/1430003969607229444-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1430003969607229444-1.mp4)

*Learn to say no*

Before accepting any new tasks, ask yourself
• Why should I do it?
• What happens if I don't do it?

Pro tip: Create a "no" folder to save the things you said no. Celebrate how much time you get back in your life.

![image](media/1430003974078402560-1.png)

*Give up precise task prioritization*

Sometimes you may be spending more time ranking the tasks than actually doing it.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1430003982328647682-1.jpg)](media/1430003982328647682-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1430003982328647682-1.mp4)

*Touch each email exactly once*

Open an email, skim through it, and make a quick decision (reply/archive/allocate a time slot). Keep your inbox zero.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1430003991103029250-1.jpg)](media/1430003991103029250-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1430003991103029250-1.mp4)

Any tips/advices on time management are very welcome!

## How to develop a productive routine?

原帖：<https://x.com/jbhuang0604/status/1570131220499148800> · 2022-09-14 · 共 7 条串文

How to develop a productive routine?

When transiting from structured learning environments (e.g., courses) to unstructured ones (e.g., research), developing a productive routine and managing their time well are critical for productivity.

Sharing some hacks I found useful! 👇

*168 hours a week*

There is time. 🕘

Thinking the time you have in terms of a week (as oppose a day) gives you new perspective on how much you could achieve.

🧮168 - (8 hrs sleep * 7 days) - (40 hrs work / week) = 72 hrs!

Make the best use of your time on what you care most!

## How to multitask?

原帖：<https://x.com/jbhuang0604/status/1643454248229785604> · 2023-04-05 · 共 10 条串文

How to multitask?

Feeling overwhelmed with multiple tasks on your to-do list? 😩

How do we effectively manage and juggle between tasks? 🤹 🤹‍♂️ 🤹‍♀️  Some tips below.👇

![image](media/1643454248229785604-1.jpg)

*Calm down*

Don't panic! With good prioritization and strategy, I am sure you will nail this!

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1643454257604214784-1.jpg)](media/1643454257604214784-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1643454257604214784-1.mp4)

*Prioritization*

Multitasking is just an illusion of rapid context switches.

To do well, identify each task's importance and urgency and develop a strategy.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1643454266059661314-1.jpg)](media/1643454266059661314-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1643454266059661314-1.mp4)

*Say no*

Are all these tasks REALLY necessary?

"No" should be your default answer.

Cut down unnecessary tasks ruthlessly and shake them off.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1643454273982799873-1.jpg)](media/1643454273982799873-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1643454273982799873-1.mp4)

*Avalanche method*

Managing multiple tasks is similar to managing credit card debt.

Avalanche: focuses on debt with the highest interest (most important tasks).

👍 More efficient/effective in the long run.
👎 Can be discouraging if the time required for the task is long.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1643454283063390208-1.jpg)](media/1643454283063390208-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1643454283063390208-1.mp4)

*Snowball method*

Snowball: focuses on debt with the smallest balance (the easiest tasks).

👍 Enjoy the little wins along the way. Feel more rewarding/motivating.
👎 Less effective toward your goal.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1643454291028393989-1.jpg)](media/1643454291028393989-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1643454291028393989-1.mp4)

*Two-minute rule*

If you can cross a task in two minutes or less, tackle it now. Don’t add that to your list of tasks.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1643454299416952832-1.jpg)](media/1643454299416952832-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1643454299416952832-1.mp4)

*Time-blocking*

Once you develop the strategy, dedicate time blocks for specific tasks, and stick to them.

Use a calendar 🗓️, not a to-do list 🗒️!

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1643454317393768449-1.jpg)](media/1643454317393768449-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1643454317393768449-1.mp4)

*Set deadlines*

Parkinson’s Law: the amount of work expands to fill the time available for its completion.

Set artificial deadlines and get more done in less time!

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1643454325195251712-1.jpg)](media/1643454325195251712-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1643454325195251712-1.mp4)

*Practice self-care*

Take care of yourself. Get enough sleep 🛌, eat well 🍲, and exercise 💪.

Prompting your body and mind to complete multiple tasks effectively.

## How to know when to stop working on a project?

原帖：<https://x.com/jbhuang0604/status/1665218942611124225> · 2023-06-04 · 共 7 条串文

How to know when to stop working on a project?

Working on a project for a while, but it does not go anywhere? 🫠

When to quit 🏳️and when to persevere 💪? 👇

*Imagine success*

Take a moment to envision the outcome of your project.

Does it make you say, "Hell yes!" or are you lukewarm?

If you're not enthusiastic about the potential success, it's time to let go.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1665218970255867905-1.jpg)](media/1665218970255867905-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1665218970255867905-1.mp4)

*Scope it down*

Very often, we get stuck because the project needs to be better-scoped.

Is it too ambitious and overwhelming?

Consider whether breaking it down into smaller, more manageable tasks is possible.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1665218976819953667-1.jpg)](media/1665218976819953667-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1665218976819953667-1.mp4)

*Validate your key assumptions*

Set up toy experiments to test your hypothesis. Not all of your ideas are going to pan out.

But if you fail, fail FAST.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1665218992271761412-1.jpg)](media/1665218992271761412-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1665218992271761412-1.mp4)

*Identify the path*

Assess your progress and the trajectory of your project.

Do you see a path toward success?

If you don't have an initial attack on a problem, it may be worth reconsidering your commitment.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1665219008084279296-1.jpg)](media/1665219008084279296-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1665219008084279296-1.mp4)

*Seek advice from mentors*

It's easy to get into a rabbit hole if you work alone.

Discuss your project with your mentors or peers and seek their perspectives.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1665219019220107266-1.jpg)](media/1665219019220107266-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1665219019220107266-1.mp4)

It's PERFECTLY OKAY to quit working on a project if it no longer aligns with your goals or lacks potential.

It will save you valuable time/energy and open up new opportunities! ✨
