## How to present your work via videos?

How to present your work with videos?

As academics, we are used to sharing and communicating our work via static text, figures, and tables.

But in many cases, videos are a much better medium for communicating your ideas clearly.

Sharing some tricks I learned below 🧵

*Meet the expectations upfront*

I used to think I should kick off the presentation by introducing myself, discussing how significant the problem is, and (lots of unrelated) related work to feel more "academic".

Be direct and specific instead!

## How to disseminate your research?

原帖：<https://x.com/jbhuang0604/status/1379448897823776769> · 2021-04-06 · 共 20 条串文

Sharing ideas on how to disseminate your research.

"I am THRILLED to share that our paper is accepted to ..."

Congrats! So what's next? No one is going to browse through the list of thousands of accepted papers. Ain't nobody got time for that.

Check out 🧵below for examples.

*Website*

Use memorable domain names for your project website so that people can easily find/share the link. No university account? That's okay. Register a new name for GitHub pages.

Examples:
• http://oops.cs.columbia.edu/
• http://crowdsampling.io/
• http://robust-cvd.github.io/

相关链接：
- <http://oops.cs.columbia.edu/>
- <http://crowdsampling.io/>
- <http://robust-cvd.github.io/>

*Acronym*

Make it easy for people to remember and refer to your work. As David Patterson said, the vowel is important.

For example, NeRF sounds waaaaaay cooler than NRF..

## How to communicate clearly?

原帖：<https://x.com/jbhuang0604/status/1520615196498214913> · 2022-05-01 · 共 7 条串文

How to communicate clearly?

Majority of our time goes into emails, slack messages, and loads of meetings.

Yet, as a student, no one taught me how to communicate well. (I must have annoyed a lot of people along the way ...😬)

Here is a template for organizing your message.

*(1) What?*

What's your key point? Please don't tell a convoluted story that graaaadually leads to your main point. Ain't nobody got time for that!

👉 Identify your main message and put them up front.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1520615204777832449-1.jpg)](media/1520615204777832449-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1520615204777832449-1.mp4)

*(2) So What?*

Why is it relevant or important for your work/project/team? Why should your audience care? 🤷‍♂️🤷‍♀️

👉 Tell me why?

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1520615212474273793-1.jpg)](media/1520615212474273793-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1520615212474273793-1.mp4)

*(3) Now What?*

What are the *actionable* steps next?
e.g., Set up another meeting? Ask for feedback? Outline a plan for a new task?

👉 Call for specific action!

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1520615220271538176-1.jpg)](media/1520615220271538176-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1520615220271538176-1.mp4)

Example: Research

1⃣ What?
Here are the results of a toy problem A.

2⃣ So what?
This is important because the experiment helps us  study only problem A and not get distracted by problem B/C.

3⃣ Now what?
I will move on to study problem B and C.

Example: Opportunity

1⃣ What?
I am interested in working with you.

2⃣ So what?
I have relevant skills, experiences, and specific ideas on how to extend your recent work X to Y and Z.

3⃣ Now what?
When would you be available so I can present these specific directions?

Happy communicating!

Having the What/So What/Now What template in mind when writing and speaking about ANYTHING has been very helpful for keeping my messages clear and concise!

Hope this helps yours too! 👍

## How to set up a good calendar invite?

原帖：<https://x.com/jbhuang0604/status/1528566229505650691> · 2022-05-23 · 共 6 条串文

How to Set Up a Good Calendar Invite?

Our days are filled with events on the calendar (e.g., project meetings, roundtable, committee, 1:1s) 🗓️

But, most calendar invites are awfully vague and lead to unproductive meetings.

Five tips on writing a good calendar invite 👇

*1⃣ Specific Title*

Nobody knows what "project update" or "internship meeting weekly" are. 🙄

Labeling your event with a specific title helps everyone!

## How to schedule a meeting?

How to schedule a meeting?

When you ask for a meeting with others, you are asking for their time. You are asking for their most valuable, finite resource to benefit yourself (e.g., for advice, networking, questions, and opportunities).

Here are some tips that I found useful.

*Provide your availability upfront*

The most annoying thing you can do is to ask:

"Can you give me a list of time slots when you are available?"

This basically asks people to put ALL their availability ON HOLD for a meeting with you.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1866260235423265077-1.jpg)](media/1866260235423265077-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1866260235423265077-1.mp4)

*Convert the timezone for them*

When you provide your availability, make it easy for others to decide and plan their days by converting the time slots into their time zones.

[![GIF 动画预览](media/1866260243178725419-1.jpg)](media/1866260243178725419-1.mp4)

▶ [GIF 动画（点击播放）](media/1866260243178725419-1.mp4)

*List specific goals of your meeting*

Tell them how the meeting can help you. Write down questions that you will ask. Describe the homework you have done. Provide background material on what you want to discuss.

Assure them that you will not waste their time.

*Send out calendar invite asap*

If an event is not on the calendar, it does not exist.

When you finalize the time, please send a calendar invite with a detailed title/content/links as soon as possible. Also, send a reminder before the meeting on what they should expect.
